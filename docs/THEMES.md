## Dynamic themes

- `?theme=random` — a stable pick per user that reshuffles every UTC midnight
- `?theme=season` — follows the calendar: nord winters, catppuccin springs,
  ember summers, gruvbox autumns, dracula for halloween week

![season](https://github-pulse-topaz.vercel.app/u/pouyashahrdami?theme=season&size=compact)
